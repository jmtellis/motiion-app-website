Do’s and don’ts

Do

  • Preserve drafts and context.
  • Use shared tokens and controls.
  • Give failures a useful recovery action.
  • Keep approval, email, identity and plans distinct.
  • Check keyboard, mobile and long content.

Don’t

  • Use color as the only signal.
  • Stack drawers or sticky regions.
  • Turn backend failures into empty states.
  • Reintroduce booking contracts or payments.
  • Add motion that delays work.

160ms purposeful motion. Reduced motion disables nonessential animation. Focus ring: 2px, visible on every control. Touch targets: 44px minimum.
